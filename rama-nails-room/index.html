Our Story

A little corner of calm & beauty in Amman

Rama Nails Room was born from a simple belief — that every woman deserves a moment that is entirely her own. Tucked away on Najiah An Nabulsi Street, our studio is a warm, welcoming space where you can slow down, breathe, and be beautifully cared for.

We treat every set of nails like a piece of art. With premium products, meticulous hygiene, and a truly personal touch, your hands and feet are always in the gentlest, most skilled care. Whether it's a quick refresh or a full pampering session, you'll leave glowing.

— with love, Rama
